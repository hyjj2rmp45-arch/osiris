## 1. CRITICAL RISK NOTICE

Trading cryptocurrencies through automated systems involves substantial risk of financial loss. This document discloses material risks associated with using OSIRIS.

## 2. MARKET RISKS

### 2.1 Price Volatility
Cryptocurrency prices are highly volatile and may fluctuate significantly within short periods. You may lose all or part of your investment.

### 2.2 Liquidity Risk
Some tokens may have insufficient liquidity, making it difficult to execute trades at desired prices or exit positions.

### 2.3 Slippage
Actual execution prices may differ from quoted prices due to market movement between quote and execution.

## 3. TECHNICAL RISKS

### 3.1 Smart Contract Risk
Tokens and protocols may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, or malicious code that could result in total loss.

### 3.2 Network Risk
Solana network congestion, outages, or upgrades may prevent trade execution or cause transaction failures.

### 3.3 MEV/ Sandwich Attacks
Other market participants may see your pending transactions and extract value from them.

### 3.4 Integration Risk
Third-party services (RPC providers, DEXs, price feeds) may fail, return incorrect data, or become unavailable.

## 4. OPERATIONAL RISKS

### 4.1 Automated Trading Risk
Automated systems may execute trades that result in losses due to:
- Incorrect configuration
- Unexpected market conditions
- Software bugs
- Delayed or failed transactions

### 4.2 Copy Trading Risk
When copy trading:
- Target wallets may change strategies without notice
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- You may copy unsuccessful trades
- Slippage and fees may reduce returns

### 4.3 Data Accuracy Risk
Price feeds, RPC data, and market data may be delayed, inaccurate, or manipulated.

## 5. REGULATORY RISKS

### 5.1 Legal Uncertainty
Cryptocurrency regulation is evolving. Changes in law may affect the Service or your ability to use it.

### 5.2 Tax Obligations
You are solely responsible for reporting and paying taxes on trading activity.

### 5.3 Sanctions Compliance
You must ensure you are not subject to sanctions or located in prohibited jurisdictions.

## 6. SECURITY RISKS

### 6.1 Private Key Security
Loss or theft of private keys results in irreversible loss of funds. OSIRIS cannot recover lost keys.

### 6.2 Phishing Risk
Always verify URLs and application sources. OSIRIS will never ask for your private keys or seed phrase.

### 6.3 Third-Party Risk
Wallets, exchanges, and other services you use may be compromised or fail.

## 7. SPECIFIC PRODUCT RISKS

### 7.1 Paper Trading
Paper trading uses simulated funds and does not reflect actual trading results. Performance in paper trading does not guarantee live trading success.

### 7.2 Circuit Breaker
The circuit breaker may trigger and halt trading during normal market conditions, preventing you from exiting positions.

### 7.3 Kill Switch
The emergency kill switch may be activated, halting all trading activity.
