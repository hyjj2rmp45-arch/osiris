# OSIRIS Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-26  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-26  

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how OSIRIS ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our non-custodial Solana trading automation platform.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Telegram User ID**: Used for authentication and notifications
- **Wallet Addresses**: Public Solana addresses for trading
- **Usage Data**: Trading preferences, configuration settings
- **Support Communications**: Messages sent to our support channels

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **IP Address**: For security and rate limiting
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system
- **Usage Metrics**: Feature usage, performance data
- **Error Logs**: For debugging and improvement

### 2.3 Information We DO NOT Collect
- Private keys or seed phrases
- Passwords (we use Telegram authentication)
- Financial account credentials
- Personal identification documents

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the Service
- Send important notifications and alerts
- Improve and optimize the Service
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:
- **Service Providers**: Hosting, analytics, and support services
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of merger or acquisition

## 5. DATA RETENTION

We retain your data only as long as necessary:
- Account data: Until account deletion
- Trading logs: 90 days (configurable)
- Security logs: 365 days
- Audit trails: As required by applicable law

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing
- Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@osiris.trading

## 7. SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive fields
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

## 8. COOKIES

We use essential cookies only for session management. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

## 9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Data may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

## 11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via the Service or Telegram notifications.

## 12. CONTACT

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@osiris.trading  
For general inquiries: support@osiris.trading
